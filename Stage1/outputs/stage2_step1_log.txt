contrast in Figure 2 was substantially an exposure contrast.

5. READING THIS STEP
======================================================================
Settled: whether the model machinery is adequate — distribution, random
effect, residual behaviour — and how far the protection contrast moves when
wave exposure is adjusted for.

Not settled: anything about environmental drivers, which are not in the model
yet; and anything causal. Protection was not assigned at random, and adjusting
for exposure removes one known confounder rather than all of them.

Decision before Step 2: habitat covariates are added next. If reserves were
PLACED in different habitat, adjusting for habitat is correct. If protection
CHANGED the habitat, habitat is a mediator and adjusting for it would remove
part of the effect being measured. For coral trout over this period the first
is far more plausible, so Step 2 adjusts. Recorded here so the choice is not
revisited after seeing which way the coefficient moves.
