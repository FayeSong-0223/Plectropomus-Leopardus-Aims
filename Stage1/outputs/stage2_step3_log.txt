3. SHELTERED SITES ONLY
======================================================================
Adjusting for exposure assumes the functional form is right and that exposure
is the only cross-sectional confounder. Restriction assumes neither. The cost
is precision, and here the cost is not small.

sites in the sheltered stratum:
            
             Fished NTR 1987 NTR 2004
  Palm            6        5        3
  Whitsunday      3       10        7

Note how thin Whitsunday's fished arm is. The restriction removes the
confound but leaves few fished sites to compare against.

5. LAGGED CYCLONE AND DISTRIBUTION SWAP
======================================================================
The Stage 1 plan asked for cyclone exposure integrated across survey intervals.
That is not possible from this extract: exposure is recorded only at survey
points, and the intervening years are absent. A lagged term — the previous
survey's value at the same site — is what the data can support, and it is a
weaker substitute. Proper integration needs external cyclone track data, which
goes on the list of questions for AIMS.
