
======================================================================
1. CONCURVITY
======================================================================
Concurvity measures how far one smooth can be reproduced by the others. It is
the GAM analogue of collinearity, it is not visible in a p-value, and it was
never computed for this model until now. Values approach 1 when a term is
nearly redundant. The 'worst' column is the pessimistic bound.

         para s(YEAR):REGIONPalm s(YEAR):REGIONWhitsunday s(rugosity) s(LHC)
worst       1              0.879                    0.914       0.644  0.750
observed    1              0.597                    0.602       0.609  0.446
estimate    1              0.481                    0.634       0.568  0.690
         s(kd490) s(maxDHW) s(Cyclone) s(SITE)
worst       0.926     0.951      0.655   1.000
observed    0.573     0.537      0.571   0.082
estimate    0.775     0.744      0.351   0.183

terms with worst-case concurvity above 0.8:
   s(SITE)  1.000
   s(maxDHW)  0.951
   s(kd490)  0.926
   s(YEAR):REGIONWhitsunday  0.914
   s(YEAR):REGIONPalm  0.879

This is the number behind a caveat stated qualitatively throughout: the thermal
and turbidity smooths are substantially reproducible from the region-specific
year trends, because both vary largely at region-year level. Their coefficients
should be read as one of several possible splits of shared variation, not as
independently identified effects.

2. BASIS DIMENSION (k.check)
======================================================================
k was fixed at 5 throughout with a stated justification. That justification was
never tested. A k-index below 1 with a small p-value means residual pattern
remains that the basis cannot represent.

                         k'     edf k-index p-value

s(LHC)                    4  2.6552  0.9762  0.5325
s(kd490)                  4  2.6847  0.8474  0.0025
s(maxDHW)                 4  3.4784  0.9120  0.1200
s(Cyclone)                4  1.8059  0.9961  0.7600
s(SITE)                  71 36.3363      NA      NA

flagged: s(YEAR):REGIONPalm, s(YEAR):REGIONWhitsunday, s(kd490)

For the year smooths this is expected and not fixable by raising k: Palm has
only six distinct survey years, so k cannot exceed six, and genuine year-to-year
jumps are not smooth in any basis. Model B, which saturates time with region-year
as a factor, is the specification that answers this, and it agrees on protection.
For kd490 the flag is a real limitation and is reported as such.

CORRECTION. Step 4 reported Whitsunday Moran's I = 0.146, p = 0.007 from a single
seed and called it spatial structure the site random effect had not absorbed.
That overstates it. Seed 1 sits near the top of the range, and the permutation
p-value crosses 0.05 in roughly half of randomisations.

What is defensible: Whitsunday Moran's I is positive under every seed tested and
Palm's is centred near zero and never significant under any. The evidence points
to residual spatial structure in Whitsunday but does not establish it at
conventional thresholds, and any statement of it must carry the seed range.
The normality of the residuals, by contrast, is robust: never rejected in 20 of 20.

======================================================================
4. DEPTH: WHY IT IS NOW A LINEAR TERM
======================================================================
depth varies at 0 of 71 sites in this subset. A penalised smooth of a
site-constant covariate is fully aliased with the site random effect, and the
concurvity check above returned exactly 1.000 for it before this change.

        specification    AIC dev_expl            W1987            W2004
1   depth as a smooth 2353.1    60.2% 3.21 (2.28-4.52) 2.89 (1.99-4.19)
2 depth linear (used) 2353.0    60.2% 3.21 (2.28-4.52) 2.89 (1.99-4.19)
3       depth omitted 2354.1    59.3% 3.62 (2.62-4.99) 3.34 (2.38-4.68)

The smooth and the linear term give identical estimates, because the smooth had
already collapsed to a straight line (edf 1.005). Omitting depth raises the
Whitsunday estimate, so depth is doing real adjustment work and should be kept.
The linear form keeps that adjustment without the aliasing.
